What Sous is

Sous is a personal recipe book with an AI cooking assistant. It is free and invitation-only: access is granted by request and approval, or by redeeming a single-use invite link sent by someone who already has access. The owner can remove access at any time. It comes with no guarantee of availability. Your recipe library lives in your account and loads when you are signed in. Cached translations of your recipes are stored in Firestore under your account and are deleted when their recipe is deleted. Translations of recipes shared with you are not stored; they are translated again each session. You can share a named collection with another admitted member by email. A viewer can see those recipes and photos. An editor can also change the recipes' details (such as title, ingredients, steps, notes, servings, cooking times, and tags), but not their photos, and cannot delete them or change who has access. Sharing does not create a household account.

Accuracy

On a recipe, that recipe's text is sent to Google's Gemini API at request time to answer your questions. When you use the library assistant (Ask, at /assistant), many recipes from your library — titles, tags, and the full text of recipes the assistant looks up — not only the recipe you are viewing, are sent to Gemini at request time to produce the reply.

The assistant, dictation, recipe translation, and the recipe importer are AI and can be wrong — including about cooking times, temperatures, substitutions, quantities and food safety, and about what you said into the microphone. Recipe text is sent to Gemini, or the configured translation provider, for translation, including at import. Recipes read from photos, especially handwriting, can have wrong or missing amounts; check them in the preview before saving. Those photos are sent to Google's Gemini API to extract the recipe and are not stored. Use your own judgement and check anything that matters.

Acceptable use

Sous is for your own recipes and normal personal use. Do not try to break or probe the service, and do not use it to generate abuse.

Ending it

You can stop at any time: sign out, clear this site's browser data on a device, or revoke Sous's access from your Google account permissions page. The UI language preference is kept in this browser's local storage under cook.locale. To remove all data stored for your account, see the Privacy policy. The service may also change or stop at any time.

Sous is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. To the extent permitted by law, no liability is accepted for any loss or damage arising from its use — including anything you cook.
