About

Sous is a personal recipe book for cooking from.

You keep recipes in an account: write them yourself, or import from a URL or pasted text. Each recipe has a readable view — ingredients, steps, servings, photos — and a cooking assistant that answers questions about that recipe, typed or spoken, while you cook.

The library lives in your account and loads when you sign in with Google. There is no on-device recipe database. Sous is invitation-only; requesting access does not grant it.
