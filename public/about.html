About

Sous is a personal recipe book for cooking from.

You keep recipes in an account: write them yourself, or import from a URL or pasted text. Each recipe has a readable view with ingredients, steps, servings, and photos.

Open a recipe and you can ask a cooking assistant about it while you cook. It can help with substitutions, technique, and timing. You can type, speak, or send a photo of how it's going. If you ask it to change the recipe, it proposes an edit you can apply to this one, or save as a new variant.

The recipe library, including photos, is synced to your account and accessible from any device.
