Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

What is stored

When you sign in, Sous sets a signed session cookie (sous_session) holding your Google account id (sub) and email address. The cookie is HttpOnly and is not readable by page scripts; it does not contain your display name. A cached copy of your profile may also be kept in this browser's local storage under cook.session, so the app can show who is signed in.

Your recipes, chat messages, cooking progress and photos stay in this browser's storage (IndexedDB) on each device. They are not uploaded to a Sous server.

No password and no Google refresh token is stored — the app only ever asks Google who you are.

What it is used for

Knowing which Google account is signed in, so chat and recipe import can run as you. Your information is not sold, not shared, and not used for advertising or profiling.

What it can reach in your Google account

Sign-in uses the scopes openid, userinfo.email and userinfo.profile — your name, email address and account id, and nothing else. Sous cannot read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or photos.

Third parties

Recipe text, your chat messages and any photo you send the assistant are passed to Google's Gemini API at request time to produce a reply. Recipe import fetches the URL you paste. Hosting is on Google Cloud. Nothing else.

Retention and deletion

Sign out to drop the session cookie — this works when the network request succeeds. If you sign out while offline, the cached profile is cleared from this browser, but the HttpOnly cookie may remain until it expires or a later sign-out succeeds. Clearing this site's browser data deletes the on-device library. You can also revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account permissions page. There is no in-app "delete my account" button in this version, because there is no server-side library yet.

Access

Sous is invitation-only: only allow-listed email addresses can sign in.
