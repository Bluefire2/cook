Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

What is stored

When you sign in, Sous associates your Google account id (sub), email address, and display name with your library. A signed session cookie (sous_session) holds your sub and email; it is HttpOnly and is not readable by page scripts. A cached copy of your profile may also be kept in this browser's local storage under cook.session, so the app can show who is signed in.

Your recipes, chat messages, cooking progress, and attached photos are stored in Google Cloud (Firestore and Cloud Storage) in the europe-west1 region. They are loaded into this browser while you are signed in. There is no on-device recipe database.

No password and no Google refresh token is stored — the app only ever asks Google who you are.

What it is used for

Showing your own recipe library across devices, and answering cooking questions. Your information is not sold, not shared, and not used for advertising.

What it can reach in your Google account

Sign-in uses the scopes openid, userinfo.email and userinfo.profile — your name, email address and account id, and nothing else. Sous cannot read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or photos.

Third parties

Recipe text, your chat messages and any photo you send the assistant are passed to Google's Gemini API at request time to produce a reply. Recipe import fetches the URL you paste. Hosting is on Google Cloud. Nothing else.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a recipe in Sous deletes its chat thread, cooking progress, and attached photos from your stored library.

To delete all data stored for your account, email chernyshov.k@gmail.com from the same address you use to sign in. There is no in-app delete-account button. Revoking Sous's access from your Google account permissions page signs you out but does not by itself delete your stored recipes.

Sign out to drop the session cookie when the network request succeeds. If you sign out while offline, the cached profile is cleared from this browser, but the HttpOnly cookie may remain until it expires or a later sign-out succeeds. Your library in Google Cloud remains until you request account deletion.

Access

Sous is invitation-only: only allow-listed email addresses can sign in.
