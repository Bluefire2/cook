Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

What is stored

When you sign in, Sous associates your Google account id (sub), email address, and display name with your library. A signed session cookie (sous_session) holds your sub and email; it is HttpOnly and is not readable by page scripts. A cached copy of your profile may also be kept in this browser's local storage under cook.session, so the app can show who is signed in.

Your recipes, chat messages, cooking progress, and attached photos are stored in Google Cloud (Firestore and Cloud Storage) in the europe-west1 region. They are loaded into this browser while you are signed in. There is no on-device recipe database.

No password and no Google refresh token is stored — the app only ever asks Google who you are.

If you complete Google sign-in but are not a member, and you press Request access, Sous stores your email address, Google display name, Google account id (sub) and the request timestamps in Firestore (europe-west1) so the owner can decide. No recipe library and no users/{sub} document is created for a person who is not admitted.

What it is used for

Showing your own recipe library across devices, and answering cooking questions. Your information is not sold, not shared, and not used for advertising.

What it can reach in your Google account

Sign-in uses the scopes openid, userinfo.email and userinfo.profile — your name, email address and account id, and nothing else. Sous cannot read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or photos.

Third parties

Recipe text, your chat messages and any photo you send the assistant are passed to Google's Gemini API at request time to produce a reply. Using Dictate uploads a short microphone recording to Sous, which sends it to Gemini to turn into text and does not store the audio. Recipe import fetches the URL you paste. If you press Request access, the notification email to the owner is sent through Resend and contains your email address, display name and the time of the request; it includes no one-click approval link — approval only ever happens on the owner's own signed-in screen. Hosting is on Google Cloud.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a recipe in Sous deletes its chat thread, cooking progress, and attached photos from your stored library.

Access records are kept as follows. A pending access request is kept until the owner decides it. A declined request is kept indefinitely, until it is deleted by hand, because that record is what stops a repeat request from notifying the owner again. An approved request is likewise kept indefinitely — it is what the owner's admin screen lists. A membership record lasts as long as the person has access; revoking access sets its status to revoked rather than deleting it, so a revoked person still has a row. There is no automated purge job: none of these records is deleted on a schedule.

To delete all data stored for your account, email chernyshov.k@gmail.com from the same address you use to sign in. There is no in-app delete-account button. A deletion request covers all of it: your access-request record, your membership record, and your stored library — recipes, chat messages, cooking progress and photos — and deleting these also removes your access. Revoking Sous's access from your Google account permissions page signs you out but does not by itself delete your stored recipes.

Sign out to drop the session cookie when the network request succeeds. If you sign out while offline, the cached profile is cleared from this browser, but the HttpOnly cookie may remain until it expires or a later sign-out succeeds. Your library in Google Cloud remains until you request account deletion.

Access

Sous is invitation-only: signing in is limited to allow-listed email addresses and to accounts the owner has approved from an access request. Requesting access does not grant it.
