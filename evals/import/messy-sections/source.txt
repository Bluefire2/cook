I baked this on a Tuesday after a long day and honestly it saved dinner.

Okay so this is my "weeknight focaccia" — don't @ me about authenticity, I know traditional focaccia proofs overnight. This one is for when you want bread TONIGHT.

Serves 4. Hands-on time is about 20 minutes; it bakes for 25.

For the dough
You'll need 500 grams of bread flour (I like King Arthur), 400 milliliters of lukewarm water, 7 grams of instant yeast (that's one packet), 2 teaspoons of fine salt, and 3 tablespoons of olive oil that goes INTO the dough.

For the topping
½ cup olive oil for the pan and dimples, a few sprigs of fresh rosemary (say 2 tablespoons chopped), and a big pinch of flaky salt — like a teaspoon of Maldon.

Method (skip the life story)

First mix the flour, water, yeast, salt, and the 3 tablespoons of oil in a bowl until it's a shaggy sticky dough. Cover and let it rise in a warm spot until doubled, about 45 minutes to an hour.

Oil a 9x13 pan with most of that ½ cup of olive oil. Dump the dough in and stretch it toward the corners. If it springs back, wait 10 minutes and stretch again.

Dimple the surface with wet fingers, drizzle the remaining oil, sprinkle rosemary and flaky salt.

Bake at 425°F / 220°C until deep golden, 20–25 minutes. Cool 10 minutes in the pan, then lift out and tear.
