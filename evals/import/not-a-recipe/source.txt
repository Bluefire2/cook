Wooden spoons are older than most of the pans we cook in. A good one is light,
does not scratch a skillet, and stays cool enough to hold while you stir polenta.
I keep three: a short one for sauces, a long one for stock, and a battered one
I refuse to throw away. None of this is a recipe. There are no ingredients, no
quantities, and nothing to bake. If you came here looking for dinner, look
somewhere else.
